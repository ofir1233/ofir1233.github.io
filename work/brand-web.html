I designed Hear's brand from zero

The challenge
Hear, an AI platform for contact centers, needed one brand for the product, the website, social and print.

The mark, the color, the type and one painted city, then the marketing site, a LinkedIn campaign, signage and merch.
Where it stands
The site runs on staging. The campaign, the station sign and the tote are mockups. A trade-show booth was built from my earlier design.

02 · The brand

A mark, nine colors, one typeface and a painted city

Every color has one job, one typeface sets every line, and every picture comes from the same city.

Read moreShow less

The same values run through the product and the site. In the paintings the colors are oil paint, not fixed values, but they are the same family: a blue sky, coral, lilac and green.

Color

  • One accent per screen. A page is not improved by more colors and tags.
  • Coral is a signature. It is used sparingly, never as a background.
  • A painted sky first. It is preferred over a flat fill of brand color.

Type

  • Sentence case. A capital only starts a sentence. No headline is set in capitals.
  • Our typeface inside the picture. The object never picks the font, and the writing is large.
  • Tight headlines. Running text keeps normal spacing.

The painted city

  • Thick oil paint, with a knife. Sharp patches with clear edges. Nothing smooth, sprayed or photographed.
  • Bright daylight. Saturated color, glass towers, a blue sky with heavy clouds, flowers and people.
  • Grown-up realism. Not a caricature and not a children's illustration.
  • The world is full, edge to edge. Only the area around the message stays open.
  • A person's point of view. A spectator in the crowd, a customer at a table. People are small or seen from behind.
  • The world writes the message. A receipt, a plane's trail, balloon letters, a headline. Never a layer on top.

The same nine colors run through Hear Design Lab. The design system has its own case study.

05 · The process

Every picture starts as one sentence

Each picture follows one order: the idea in words, three gray compositions, one painting, then a check at the size of a real post.

Read moreShow less

No picture is made before its idea and its composition are chosen, and the work moves one picture at a time.

The brand came ahead of the website and the product in my plan for Hear's redesign. Its eight phases are Research, Branding, Designing, Website, Growth, Product, UI design and Process.

  1. The idea, in wordsWhat you see, where the words live in the scene, and what a stranger understands about Hear.
  2. Three gray compositionsGray shapes in the final format, with one color for the place of the words.
  3. One paintingPainted only after a composition is chosen, then fixed one problem at a time.
  4. A check at feed sizeInside a mockup of the feed, at the size a post is really seen.
