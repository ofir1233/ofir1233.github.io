Case study · Brand & web

I designed Hear's brand from zero

The challenge
Hear needed a brand from zero, one that could carry from the product to the website, print and trade-show floors.
What I designed
Its color, typography, imagery and graphics, then a new marketing site that I designed and built in two directions.
Outcome
The brand went to trade-show booths and into print, signage and merch mockups, and its colors became ramps in the Design Lab's Storybook. Both site directions are previews.
Role
Brand design · site design & build
When
Since 2023 · site in preview
Scope
Identity, website, print, signage, merch, booths

01 · The brand

Color, type, imagery, graphics

Boards from the brand, each with its rules written down in its own words.

Read moreShow less
Color
“Grounding neutrals (Charcoal, Light Grey, Pearl), supportive mid-tones (Deep Teal, Horizon Dust, Balance Sage), and pops of vibrancy (Pulse Coral, Signal Green, Aura Lilac, Cobalt Blue).”
Type
“A rounded sans serif typeface in seven weights with corresponding italics.” It is “chunky, happy and a bit retro”, especially the heavier weights.
Imagery
“Serious professional and moments of human warmth or creativity sparks.”

In the Design Lab: Pulse Coral and Charcoal are color ramps in the Design Lab's Storybook. The design system has its own case study.

02 · The site

A new site, built in two directions

I designed and built two directions of the same story. Both are previews.

The new marketing site puts the brand in motion: a thesis that reveals itself as you scroll, a globe where nothing goes unheard, and a clear line from problem to platform.

Dark, cinematic

Direction B
