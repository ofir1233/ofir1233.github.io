Case study · Hear Design Lab

I rebuilt Hear’s live product in code, so every fix had to beat the real screen

Hear is an AI platform for contact centers. I copied its screens faithfully, then made my redesigns compete with the copies, on the same data.

For
Hear’s product team and developers, who decide what gets built.
The problem
A redesign judged against a picture of the old screen always wins.
What I did
Hear Design Lab, my working copy of Hear, built in code: each screen as shipped, beside my fix.
Where it stands
In Hear’s own repository since Mar 2026. Topics, its colours and icons shipped; most fixes wait.

The problem

Against a picture of the old screen, a redesign always wins

Beside a tidy mockup of the old screen, a new design always looks better, so mine had to face a faithful copy of the live screen, on the same data, at the same size.

Alerts, side by side

The fix that moved Alerts’ list up lost. The one that kept the page whole won.

Moving the list up took the numbers and the chart off the page, so the winner keeps production’s page whole and makes its table the chart’s key. The list still sits low: on a 1440×900 screen, not one monitor shows above the fold. (Alerts are checks the team sets, which fire on a call; each check is a monitor.)

MonitorsActivity

Deleted: the two directions that moved the list up

Two tabs, monitors first · The list only

The tabs were my own recommendation after the research. Both took the numbers and the chart off the page. The monitor’s own page survived, as where a row opens.

What the test removed

The side-by-side test killed my own fixes first

On 28 Oct 2026 every change was checked again against the live screen, side by side at the same height. An item not clearly better was removed, and production’s version came back. These were mine.

What copying found

Copying the real screen found what no mockup shows

The fold and the 50-call total were two. Copying screens at real widths and in Hebrew turned up more, and each got a fix in the Lab.

Compliance · the period

East of UTC, the date chip is a day early

PLATFORM📅︎ 2026-09-27 – 2026-10-27A cell on the same range: Sep 28Below 1280 px the chip hides under its clip.
FIXEDSep 28 – Oct 28, 2026Always on screen, in the reader’s own day.

The chip printed each end of the range in UTC, so east of UTC it showed the day before.

Ask AI

With AI, I can’t design the answer’s words, only its shape

The Lab’s Ask AI panel calls a real model, so no two answers are worded alike. I designed the shape every answer takes and the states around it. Starter prompts never quote a figure, and Send turns into Stop while an answer is written.
