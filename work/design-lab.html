Case study · Hear Design Lab

I rebuilt Hear’s live product in code, so every fix had to beat the real screen

Hear is an AI platform for contact centers. I copied its screens faithfully, then made my redesigns compete with the copies, on the same data.

For
Hear’s product team and developers, who decide what gets built.
The problem
A redesign judged against a picture of the old screen always wins.
What I did
Hear Design Lab, my working copy of Hear, built in code: each screen as shipped, beside my fix.
Where it stands
In Hear’s repository since 16 Mar 2026. Topics, its colours and icons shipped; most fixes wait.

Alerts, side by side

Moving Alerts’ list above the fold lost. Making its table the chart’s key won.

Alerts are checks a team sets on calls; each is a monitor. My first fix pulled the list above the fold and put the four numbers and the chart behind a tab. Beside the real screen, losing them cost more than one scroll. The fix that won leaves the fold and makes the table the chart’s key.

What the test removed

The side-by-side test killed my own fixes first

On 28 Oct 2026 every change was checked again against the live screen, side by side at the same height. An item not clearly better was removed, and production’s version came back. Five of my own changes went: would you have kept them?

  2. Alerts

    What fired, first

    Activity numbers before setup numbers, with the monitors folded into “8 of 9 active”.

    Why the review removed it

    A monitor count is setup, not activity. Production’s split was right.

  3. Alerts

    Say what the cap counts

    Total monitors 9Active monitors 8 / 10

    Production shows “8 / 10”, eight active of a cap of ten, beside nine monitors in all. I labelled the pill.

    Why the review removed it

    It didn’t solve the confusion: two totals, 9 and 10, under the same words.

  4. Alerts

    Every applied filter in sight

    The default filter as its own chip, not hidden behind “+1”.

    Why the review removed it

    Not clearly better in review; production’s own filter came back.

  5. Knowledge

    A panel as tall as its content

    The document tree’s panel ends where its list ends.

    Why the review removed it

    A matter of taste, not a visible improvement.

The review kept none of the five.

What copying found

Copying the real screen found what no mockup shows

Real widths and Hebrew turned up more, and each got a fix in the Lab.

Compliance · the period

East of UTC, the date chip is a day early

PLATFORMCreated At 2026-09-27 - 2026-10-28The table’s first day: Sep 28Below 1280 px the chip hides under its clip.
FIXEDSep 28 – Oct 28, 2026Always on screen, in the reader’s own day.

The chip printed each end of the range in UTC, so east of UTC the range started the day before.

Ask AI

With AI, I can’t design the answer’s words, only its shape

The Lab’s Ask AI panel calls a real model, so no two answers are worded alike. I designed the shape every answer takes and the states around it. Suggested questions, shown before you ask, never quote a figure.
