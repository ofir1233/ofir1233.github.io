Case study · Mobile app · Reimagined in 2026

FYI, a messenger for people who make things

Think WhatsApp for creative crews, with AI inside the conversation, group work, and events you can promote from the chat. It never launched. What plays on this page is a 2026 reimagining.

The challenge
The work around a creative project is mostly conversation, so the plan, the files and the launch should live where the chat already is.
What I designed
FYI (Focus Your Ideas), a dark iOS messenger for creators with an AI assistant, tasks, voice, video calls and group projects, from my first year at Intune, before Hear.
Outcome
FYI began as Intune's collaboration with will.i.am of the Black Eyed Peas, and it did not go ahead. In 2026 I reimagined it, and the screens below run as live code. The original Figma screens close the page.

Four rules I set for the reimagining
  1. The conversation is the canvas.Tasks, files, events and AI results live in the thread as objects, and each one is also listed in its own tab.
  2. AI says who can see it.Ask it alone and only you see the answer, and posting it is your choice. Ask it in the group, or turn on call notes, and everyone sees it under a label that says so.
  3. Suggest, never assume.AI can propose a task, an owner or a date. A person confirms it, and a missing date stays empty.
  4. Color says who is acting.Blue is you, gray is everyone else, violet is AI, teal is an event, green is live.

Edge cases

  • A transcript folds away with one tap. The audio stays the source.
  • Read receipts keep the original message info: Read by and Delivered to, per person.
03 · Day 3, 8:02 AM · AI in the conversation

Catch up without scrolling back

Sixty-four messages later, one tap catches Maya up, and every fact points back to its source.

What changed from the originalShow less

In the original, the AI assistant opened as its own chat with suggested prompts, and an AI button sat in the attachments sheet. In the reimagining it works on the thread itself, in two modes: private to you, like this catch-up, or in the group when someone asks it something. When it drafts a message, the person edits it and sends it as their own.

Reimagined in 2026 · demo content

On the phone: a private catch-up with sources, a draft Maya sends as a normal message, and an answer the whole group sees.

In the original Figma file: the New AI assistant chat with suggested prompts, and the AI button in the attachments sheet. See 10 and 09.

Decision · 2026 reimagining

Is AI a contact or a layer?

  • A contact in the chat listFamiliar, but its answers look like a member's message.
  • A layer in the threadFull-width cards with no avatar, a violet hairline and a label that says who can see it.

Why: in a group, nobody should have to guess whether a person or a model said something.

Edge cases
  • Catch me up appears only past 30 unread, because below that, reading is faster.
  • A summary that leaves something out says so: “Couldn't read 1 voice note.”
  • A deleted source shows “Message deleted”, and its line is marked.
  • An AI draft carries “Drafted with AI” until it is sent. After that it is the person's own message.
04 · Day 3, 8:20 AM · Message to task

A promise in the chat becomes a task

Theo promises a cut by Nov 12. FYI offers to make it a task, and Maya confirms.

What changed from the originalShow less

The original put New Task in the attachments sheet, next to Camera and Documents, with a name, a due date, assignees and a status. The reimagining starts from the message instead: AI fills the same fields from Theo's words and only suggests the owners, and nothing becomes a task until a person taps the check. The task stays attached to its message, so the conversation and the plan never drift apart.

Decision · 2026 reimagining

Where does a task start?

  • From the attachments sheet, as in the originalA blank sheet, and the context gets typed again.
  • From the message that asked for itThe fields come from its words, and the attachments sheet keeps New Task for work that starts from scratch.

Why: the reason for a task is in the conversation, so the task should carry it.

Edge cases
  • With no date in the message, the date stays empty and marked. AI doesn't guess.
  • If two people make the same message a task, the second sees “Maya already made this a task” and opens it.
  • An edited message shows “Source edited” on its task.
  • The suggestion offers once. Dismissing it hides it for that message.
05 · Day 19, 7:05 PM · Together

One thread, three phones, in sync

Ines posts three covers, the crew votes, and Theo shares the cut on a call. Every phone keeps up.

A group chat is never one screen, so I show one moment from three phones: your bubbles are blue, everyone else's are not, and the votes land on all three at once. When the call ends, its notes post to the thread for everyone.

Decision · 2026 reimagining

When do call notes run?

  • Always onUseful, but nobody chose it.
  • Only for whoever turns them onThat means private notes on a shared call.
  • On for the call, visible to allA label on every screen says Notes on, and anyone can turn them off.

Why: notes about a group should never be a secret from the group.

Edge cases
  • A phone that falls behind shows “Sync paused” instead of a stale frame.
  • Late joiners see notes from the moment they joined.
  • Anyone on the call can switch notes off for everyone.
  • A vote stays open until the person who asked closes it.
06 · Day 24, 6:10 PM · Launch night

Make it an event, then tell everyone

Kofi's message becomes an event. One switch makes it public, and one tap cuts a story, a post and a link page.

What changed from the originalShow less

Group info in the original had a Public project switch at the top and a Shared Events list. This is where I took those two ideas furthest. AI drafts the event from the chat, a host checks it, and promotion makes the formats a creator needs, with RSVPs coming back to the same thread. The formats are views of the event, not copies, so a change in one place reaches all of them.

Reimagined in 2026 · demo content

Try it

Restyle the poster or move the doors. The phone and all three formats follow, because they are views of one event.

On the phone: FYI AI drafts the event from Kofi's message, Maya promotes it, RSVPs come back, and launch night starts.

In the original Figma file: the Public project switch and Shared Events in group info. See 15.

Decision · 2026 reimagining

Are promo formats copies or views?

  • CopiesExport once, and they drift the moment the event changes.
  • ViewsTitle, date and place are bound to the event, and a change flags every format.

Why: a launch needs one source of truth, and the chat already holds it.

Edge cases
  • A change to the date, time or place notifies each RSVP once, with the old value struck through.
  • Formats stay “Out of date” until a host refreshes them. Nothing republishes on its own.
  • AI drafts never go public without a host's tap.
  • A full event opens a waitlist.
07 · The system

What stayed FYI, and what grew up

Same true black, same speech-bubble mark, and Chat_Blue one step deeper. AI and events got colors of their own.

What changed from the originalShow less

The original design system named its colors Dark_1000, Light_1000, Chat_Blue, Green Active and Components Purple, and the reimagining keeps all five names. Chat_Blue went one step deeper so white text on a bubble passes 4.5:1, and Components Purple became the color only AI may use. The teal from the original splash screen now means an event. Every live phone on this page is built from these tokens, so the spec below describes the same parts that play above.

What stayed
  • True black, the doodle wallpaper and heavy uppercase headlines
  • The outlined speech-bubble mark
  • Chat_Blue for your words, Green Active for who is here, a violet AI glyph
  • Chat, Tasks and Project tabs, with Approved, In progress and Finished
  • Sharing and Sync, the Public switch and Shared Events
  • Role and skills, Read by, Delivered to and Transcription
What grew
  • AI moves into every thread, private by default, with sources
  • Tasks start from the message that asked
  • Events become a message type with a poster and promotion
  • Teal means event
  • Your name no longer prints inside your own bubbles
  • A new tab bar: Chats, Projects, Events, Calls, AI

Decision · 2026 reimagining

What earns a tab?

  • Keep the original orderAI, Calls, Projects, Chats, Settings.
  • Chats, Projects, Events, Calls, AIWith Settings behind your avatar.

Why: chats are home, so they come first. Events needed a place of their own, and since AI now works in every thread, its tab holds the private assistant.

Motion

One rule: an object grows from the message it came from (pill to card, bubble to task sheet, ticket to formats) and shrinks back into it. Messages enter in 320 ms, sheets in 340 ms, and only live states loop: typing, recording, speaking, streaming and the AI knot while it thinks. Screens change with a push or a cut, never a crossfade.
