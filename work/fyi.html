FYI, a messenger for people who make things

Think WhatsApp for creative crews, with AI inside the conversation, group work, and events you can promote from the chat.

The challenge
The work around a creative project is mostly conversation, so the plan, the files and the launch should live where the chat already is.
What I designed
FYI (Focus Your Ideas), a dark iOS messenger for creators with an AI assistant, tasks, voice, video calls and group projects, from my first year at Intune, before Hear.
Outcome
FYI began as Intune's collaboration with will.i.am of the Black Eyed Peas, and it did not go ahead. The screens on this page run as live code.

03 · Day 3, 8:02 AM · AI in the conversation

Catch up without scrolling back

Sixty-four messages later, one tap catches Maya up, and every fact points back to its source.

Latest version · demo content

On the phone: a private catch-up with sources, a draft Maya sends as a normal message, and an answer the whole group sees.

Decision

Is AI a contact or a layer?

  • A contact in the chat listFamiliar, but its answers look like a member's message.
  • A layer in the threadFull-width cards with no avatar, a violet hairline and a label that says who can see it.

Why: in a group, nobody should have to guess whether a person or a model said something.

Edge cases
  • Catch me up appears only past 30 unread, because below that, reading is faster.
  • A summary that leaves something out says so: “Couldn't read 1 voice note.”
  • A deleted source shows “Message deleted”, and its line is marked.
  • An AI draft carries “Drafted with AI” until it is sent. After that it is the person's own message.

On the phone: FYI suggests a task from Theo's message and fills it from Theo's words. Maya confirms, and the Tasks tab updates.

Decision

Where does a task start?

  • From the attachments sheetA blank sheet, and the context gets typed again.
  • From the message that asked for itThe fields come from its words, and the attachments sheet keeps New Task for work that starts from scratch.

Why: the reason for a task is in the conversation, so the task should carry it.

Edge cases
  • With no date in the message, the date stays empty and marked. AI doesn't guess.
  • If two people make the same message a task, the second sees “Maya already made this a task” and opens it.
  • An edited message shows “Source edited” on its task.
  • The suggestion offers once. Dismissing it hides it for that message.

Decision

When do call notes run?

  • Always onUseful, but nobody chose it.
  • Only for whoever turns them onThat means private notes on a shared call.
  • On for the call, visible to allA label on every screen says Notes on, and anyone can turn them off.

Why: notes about a group should never be a secret from the group.

Edge cases
  • A phone that falls behind shows “Sync paused” instead of a stale frame.
  • Late joiners see notes from the moment they joined.
  • Anyone on the call can switch notes off for everyone.
  • A vote stays open until the person who asked closes it.
06 · Day 24, 6:10 PM · Launch night

Make it an event, then tell everyone

Kofi's message becomes an event. One switch makes it public, and one tap cuts a story, a post and a link page.

Latest version · demo content

Try it

Restyle the poster or move the doors. The phone and all three formats follow, because they are views of one event.

On the phone: FYI AI drafts the event from Kofi's message, Maya promotes it, RSVPs come back, and launch night starts.

Decision

Are promo formats copies or views?

  • CopiesExport once, and they drift the moment the event changes.
  • ViewsTitle, date and place are bound to the event, and a change flags every format.

Why: a launch needs one source of truth, and the chat already holds it.

Edge cases
  • A change to the date, time or place notifies each RSVP once, with the old value struck through.
  • Formats stay “Out of date” until a host refreshes them. Nothing republishes on its own.
  • AI drafts never go public without a host's tap.
  • A full event opens a waitlist.
07 · The system

One system behind every screen

True black, the speech-bubble mark and Chat_Blue for your words. AI and events have colors of their own.

Foundations
  • True black, the doodle wallpaper and heavy uppercase headlines
  • The outlined speech-bubble mark
  • Chat_Blue for your words, Green Active for who is here, a violet AI glyph
  • Chat, Tasks and Project tabs, with Approved, In progress and Finished
  • Sharing and Sync, the Public switch and Shared Events
  • Role and skills, Read by, Delivered to and Transcription
Key ideas
  • AI works in every thread, private by default, with sources
  • Tasks start from the message that asked
  • Events are a message type with a poster and promotion
  • Teal means event
  • Your own bubbles carry no name
  • A tab bar: Chats, Projects, Events, Calls, AI

Decision

What earns a tab?

  • AI firstAI, Calls, Projects, Chats, Settings.
  • Chats, Projects, Events, Calls, AIWith Settings behind your avatar.

Why: chats are home, so they come first. Events needed a place of their own, and since AI now works in every thread, its tab holds the private assistant.

Motion

One rule: an object grows from the message it came from (pill to card, bubble to task sheet, ticket to formats) and shrinks back into it. Messages enter in 320 ms, sheets in 340 ms, and only live states loop: typing, recording, speaking, streaming and the AI knot while it thinks. Screens change with a push or a cut, never a crossfade.
