The whole app

Every screen, flow by flow

The story above follows one thread. These are the screens around it, each with the rule it was designed to.

01Start

Setup ends with a crew and a project

  1. WelcomeOne line says what the app is for.

  2. ProfileSkills are asked once. They help suggest who owns a task.

  3. CrewA crew is picked from people already on FYI.

  4. New projectA project is a name, a colour and a crew.

02Chat

The thread carries the work

  1. ChatsProjects and people share one list.

  2. Voice noteA voice note gets its transcript under it.

  3. Catch upOnly you see it. Every line names its message.

  4. Ask in the groupAn answer in the group says who asked.

03Task

A promise becomes a task

  1. SuggestionThe AI offers under the message. One tap dismisses it.

  2. New taskEvery field says where it came from, or that it was not in the message.

  3. In the threadThe task stays in the chat, under the message that made it.

  4. TasksThree states: In progress, Approved, Finished.

04Decide

The crew decides in the thread

  1. CallA call starts from the chat and ends back in it.

  2. PollThe poll is a message. Votes show as they land.

  3. Call notesThe notes are there for everyone, with what comes next.

05Event

A message becomes a night out

  1. New eventDrafted from the chat. Each field says its source.

  2. InvitationThe crew answers in the thread.

  3. PromoteOne event makes a public page and three formats.

  4. The nightOn the night, the event sits on top of the chat.
