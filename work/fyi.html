Case study · FYI

From a voice note to a full room

FYI (Focus Your Ideas) is a messenger for creative crews. I designed it for iOS at Intune, before Hear. It began as Intune's collaboration with will.i.am of the Black Eyed Peas, and it did not go ahead.

Follow the crew

The phone runs as live code. The crew, Copper Hours, is demo content.
