Case study · Actions

Every finding from a call gets an owner and an outcome

Hear's AI reads contact-center calls and raises findings. I designed Actions, the board where each finding becomes a task someone owns and closes with a reason.

For
The contact-center team that follows up on what Hear's AI finds in its calls.
The problem
A finding says what happened, not who is on it or how it ended.
What I did
Designed and built the Actions board, and the task panel each card opens.
Where it stands
A redesign of the board Hear ships, in Hear Design Lab, my working copy of Hear; not built yet.
The real screen, with demo data.

The setting

Between a flagged call and a fixed problem, someone has to pick it up

  1. CallsCustomers call; agents, the people who answer the calls, take them all day.
  2. Hear's AI flags somethingAlerts: checks the team sets, which fire on a call. Agent evaluations: scores for the person who took the call.
  3. My screensA task on the boardIts source, priority, evidence and owner.
  4. A person closes itIn Done or Cancelled, with a reason.

The problem

A finding says something happened, not who is dealing with it

Open the task

Open a card, and the task holds everything needed to finish it

The refund task, open beside the board: who has it, where it came from, what to do, how it ends, and the team's thread.

The card closes it too.
  1. 1Where it came from: the Refund refused alert.
  2. 2Who has it: Daniel Brooks.
  3. 3What to do: a checklist, all three steps ticked.
  4. 4How it ends: the outcome, then Close as Resolved.
  5. 5The team's thread: @ a teammate, or @AI, on the task.

One tap leaves the task where you're needed: the refund, where Maya Collins wrote “@Alex Ward second refund complaint on this route this week.”

Why: a mention means someone needs you, as Alex Ward is needed in the refund thread. Mentions me shows those tasks first.

Close reasons are the product's own: Resolved, Duplicate, Not actionable, Escalated, Other. Hear already asks for one when a task closes, and reports on them. My part is the reason on every closed card, and in Flow a tally at the top of Done.

Priority in ink, and you see first where you're needed.

Where it stands: Hear already has an Actions board; this redesign runs in the Lab on demo data and is not built. My part: I designed and built it with Claude Code, from a first board on 30 March 2026 to five directions on 6 October.
