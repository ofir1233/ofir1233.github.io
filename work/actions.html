Case study · Actions

Every finding from a call gets an owner and an outcome

Hear's AI flags problems on contact-center calls through alerts, signals and agent evaluations. I designed Actions, the board where each finding becomes a task that someone owns and closes with a reason.

For
The contact-center team that follows up on what Hear's AI finds in its calls.
The problem
A finding says something happened. It doesn't say who is dealing with it, or how it ended.
What I did
Designed and built the Actions board and its task panel, and earlier AI Tasks, where a person reviews what the AI proposes.
Where it stands
Mar–Oct 2026, in Hear Design Lab, my working replica of Hear, with demo data.
The real screen, with demo data.

The setting

Between a flagged call and a fixed problem, someone has to pick it up

Hear is an AI platform for contact centers. Its AI finds problems on calls; Actions turns each one into work for a person.

  1. CallsAgents, the people who answer customer calls, take calls all day.
  2. Hear's AI flags somethingAn alert fires when a monitor trips; a signal classifies calls; an evaluation scores an agent.
  3. My screensA task on the boardWith its source, a priority, the line from the call and an owner.
  4. A person closes itIn Done or Cancelled, with a reason the team can look back on.

The problem

A finding says something happened, not who is dealing with it

Three questions the person working the board brings

  1. Why is this on my board?The card names its source and quotes the call.1It arrives with its evidence
  2. What do I do about it?The task opens with its call and a checklist.2It is worked from the call
  3. How did it end?A closed task keeps what was done, and why.3It closes with how it ended

How it works

One task, from the call that raised it to how it ended

The card comes in with the customer's words and leaves with what was done.

1 of 3

Close reasons

Closed work keeps its reason, so the team can learn from it

Done or Cancelled, a task says why it ended. I built the board around how teams follow up, so the team can look back at what became of each finding.

  • ResolvedRefunded · coaching set for Oct 15.
  • EscalatedHanded on, with what came back: Ground team found the bag, Oct 6.
  • DuplicatePoints to the task it repeats: Same failure as the Oct 10 alert.
  • Not actionableFeedback on slow website search: Not about a call.

Earlier work · AI Tasks, June 2026

In AI Tasks, the AI proposes and a person decides, one group at a time

Actions starts from findings. AI Tasks, a coded prototype in the Lab, starts from the AI's own proposals, such as a change to the setup.

A choice inside it: I removed an earlier risk rating, so the person judges the change itself, from its old and new values and the AI's reasoning.

The key decision

Being mentioned gets its own filter, so you find where you're needed first

Inside the Assignee menu Set aside

On the first board, mentions were one option in the Assignee dropdown, out of sight until opened.

Its own filter, Mentions me Chosen

Beside Everyone and Assigned to me, at the start of the filter bar.

Why: a mention means someone needs you on that task. Mentions me shows those tasks before you open anything.

Where it stands

The board runs in Hear Design Lab, my working replica of Hear, rebuilt in code with demo data; screens from October 2026. AI Tasks is a June 2026 prototype. I also rebuilt the Alerts page, one source of tasks; it is not covered here.

My part
