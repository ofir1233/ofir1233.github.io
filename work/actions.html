  1. 01

    A finding alone goes nowhere

    Hear’s AI raises findings from calls: an alert the team set up, or a score for the agent who took the call. Here, a customer asked twice for a refund on Oct 13 and was offered only a voucher, so the Refund refused alert fired. But a finding only says what happened. It doesn’t say who owns it, or how it ended.

  2. 02

    Every finding becomes a task

    On my board, each finding is a card with three things: where it came from, the customer’s own words with their time in the call, and who owns it. Until someone takes it, the card says Not assigned. Cards move from Pending to In progress, then end in Done or Cancelled.

  3. 03

    You see where you’re needed first

    In Hear today, to see tasks that mention you, you open the Assignee menu, switch it to Mentioned and find your own face. My first board, on 30 March 2026, kept that. In this version I made Mentions me its own filter, beside Assigned to me, because a mention means someone needs you. One tap takes Alex Ward to the refund task, where Maya Collins asked for him.

  4. 04

    The task has what you need to finish it

    Open the card and you see the alert it came from, its owner, and the call itself, with the two moments the customer asked for a refund. Daniel Brooks listens, refunds the customer, books coaching for the agent, writes the outcome in one line and closes it as Resolved. The team comments on the task itself: @ a teammate, or @AI.

  5. 05

    Closed work keeps the reason

    Hear already asks for a reason when a task closes: Resolved, Duplicate, Not actionable, Escalated or Other. I show that reason on every closed card, so the board itself says how the work ended. The refund card lands in Done with its outcome: refunded, coaching set for Oct 15.

Where it stands

In the product today: the board, mentions inside the Assignee menu, and a reason asked when a task closes.

My proposal, on demo data, not built in the product: Mentions me as its own filter, and the close reason shown on every closed card.

How I made it: working pages built with Claude Code. A first board on 30 March 2026, then five directions on 6 October 2026. This flow is the one I chose.
