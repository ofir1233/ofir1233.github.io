Case study · FYI, iOS app

From a voice note to a full room.

FYI is a social platform for creators. A crew makes the work together, shares it and announces the night, with AI working inside the conversation. I designed it at Intune, before Hear.

Maya sends a voice note. AI transcribes it, and the crew picks the idea up.

Kofi asks for a cover and AI makes the task from his words. The crew votes, and cover B becomes the sleeve.

Kofi books the room. AI drafts the event from his message, Maya publishes it, and the room fills.
